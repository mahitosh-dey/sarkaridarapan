## P2. Recover indexability. Days 1 to 2

Bing flagged 16 pages carrying a meta robots noindex. Some are intentional,
some are not, and right now every page reads noindex because of the outage, so
this can only be audited after P1.

**The one that matters most:** `/sarkari-naukri/ssc-chsl-2026` is noindex, and
"ssc chsl notification 2026" is the single highest-cited query in Bing's AI
report at 1,600 citations and 28.8 per cent citation share. The site's best
performing page is currently telling search engines to drop it.

Others on the list worth checking: `ration-card-maharashtra-2026` (236 AI
citations), `ration-card-uttar-pradesh-2026`, `pm-kisan-samman-nidhi-pm-kisan-2026`,
`pradhan-mantri-jan-dhan-yojana-pmjdy-2026`, `aai-je-atc-2026`, `kpsc-kas-2026`,
`pm-rozgar-mela-2026`.

Tasks:

1. Separate the three causes: the outage fallback, the notification-status
   robots policy in `lib/notification-status.ts`, and stale slugs that now 404.
   Some of those URLs look like old slug formats.
2. Make every genuine content page indexable again.
3. Convert the `?category=` filtered views (`/entrance-exams?category=law`,
   `?category=medical`, `?category=engineering`, `?category=science`,
   `/sarkari-yojana?category=agriculture`, `/sarkari-naukri?category=railway`)
   from noindex to a **canonical pointing at the base listing**. A canonical
   consolidates signals. A noindex just spends crawl budget to say "ignore me".
4. Check that the sub-threshold `/state/*` pages, currently `noindex, nofollow`,
   are not linked prominently in the internal link graph.

Point 3 and 4 are the direct lever on the 112 "Discovered, currently not
indexed". Google stops queueing URLs that lead nowhere.

## P3. Data correctness. Days 2 to 3

Run `scripts/day32-1-conflation-audit.mjs`. Written, committed, never run.

It checks every row for an exam date that ends before its own application
window closes, which is a signature of data belonging to a previous
recruitment cycle. Three pages have already been caught this way: AAI JE ATC,
RRB Technician, and DSSSB, where the stored exam window actually belonged to
advertisement 03/25 rather than 03/2026.

Fix whatever it finds. In a vertical where people plan their lives around
these dates, a wrong deadline is exactly what "low value content" means.

## Monetisation view, 2026-09-04

Do not apply to Microsoft or anyone else this week.

Two separate reasons, and the second one outlasts the first.

**Reason one, temporary:** the site currently serves empty pages. A reviewer
would see "Coming Soon". That is exactly the failure that produced the AdSense
rejection on 2026-07-07.

**Reason two, structural:** there is not yet enough traffic for ad revenue to
be worth the application. 257 clicks a month is roughly 8 a day. Display
revenue in India runs at low single-digit dollars per thousand pageviews at
best, so the honest projection at current traffic is well under a dollar a
month from any network. Meaningful display income needs tens of thousands of
pageviews a month, and the site is at a small fraction of that.

The bottleneck is traffic, not monetisation. Applying now spends a rejection
window on revenue that would not be noticeable.

**What is worth doing:** joining the Microsoft pubCenter waitlist carries no
rejection risk, unlike an approval review, so it can be done at any time.
pubCenter has been US only with a waitlist for other countries based on 2023
and 2024 sources, and the 2026 position for India is unconfirmed, so check the
signup page directly.

**Why Microsoft rather than AdSense when the time comes:** the traffic is Bing
traffic. 257 clicks and 21,300 impressions a month on Bing against zero on
Google. Microsoft's network monetises exactly that audience. Revisit once Bing
clicks are in the low thousands per month.
