## Post 3 — PM Kisan 23rd instalment released

🌾 PM Kisan 23rd instalment released 20 June 2026: Rs 18,880 crore to 9.4 crore farmers

Cumulative disbursed since scheme launch: Rs 4.46 lakh crore. If your account has not credited yet, the 3 most common blockers in 2026 are:

1. AgriStack Farmer ID not seeded (now compulsory in 14 states)
2. Aadhaar-bank seeding mismatch (fix at your bank, not on PM Kisan portal)
3. eKYC pending (biometric or OTP mode at CSC)

State top-ups are stacking on top of Rs 6,000 central: Rs 12,000 in Odisha, Rs 12,000 in MP, Rs 15,000 in AP, Rs 10,000 in Chhattisgarh.

## Post 5 — PMGKAY fortified rice suspension

🌾 PMGKAY: fortified rice pause since Feb 2026

5 kg per person per month free ration scheme is running until Dec 2028 (Rs 2.27 lakh crore Budget 2026-27 allocation). What changed in February 2026: fortified rice supply is paused after a technical committee review of iron-fortification thresholds.

Regular non-fortified rice continues under PDS. eKYC and ONORC (One Nation One Ration Card) rules are unchanged. If your ration card shows "seeded but not linked", the fix is at your PDS FPS shop, not the state food-supply portal.
