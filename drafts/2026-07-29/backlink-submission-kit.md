# SarkariDarapan — Backlink Submission Kit

Copy-paste everything below into each directory. **Consistency is the whole point:** use the exact same business name, URL, and contact everywhere, or the citations hurt more than they help.

## How to use this kit
1. Do **10 to 15 submissions per day**, not 200 in one go. A slow, steady link velocity looks natural; a sudden dump looks manipulative.
2. **Vary the description.** Rotate between the short, medium, and long versions below so every listing is not identical. Directories that detect duplicate text reject or devalue the listing.
3. Keep the **name, URL, email, and social links identical** on every site.
4. After each submission, fill the tracking table at the bottom (date, live?, dofollow?). We review it weekly against DR.
5. Most Tier 3 and 4 links are nofollow and low authority. They help brand citations and getting pages **discovered/crawled** (useful for your indexing problem), but real DR growth comes from the editorial outreach track, not these.

## 7. Weekly review (do this every Friday)
1. Count links marked Live in the tracking sheet.
2. Check Ahrefs referring domains delta versus last week.
3. Note which Tier 1 links (Medium, LinkedIn, GitHub) landed, since those move the needle most.
4. Anything still "submitted, not live" after 2 weeks: re-check or drop it.

Reminder: this kit builds the **foundation** citations. The links that actually raise DR toward the 30+ target are editorial mentions from education and exam-prep blogs that already link to SarkariResult and FreeJobAlert. That outreach is the separate, higher-value track.
